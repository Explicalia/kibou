Dental health

Professional ultrasonic dental cleaning for dogs and cats.

How it’s done

Carried out by registered veterinarians. It is not a grooming service or a cosmetic clean.

  1. Check-up first

    • A veterinary examination before the treatment.
    • A blood test if needed.
    • An assessment of your pet for the anaesthetic.

  2. Ultrasonic cleaning

    • Veterinary ultrasonic equipment.
    • Tartar removed above and below the gum line.
    • Professional scaling.

  3. Extractions, only if needed

    • Damaged teeth are assessed.
    • With advanced periodontitis, some may need to come out.
    • Always done by the vet.
  • General anaesthetic
  • Takes 30 minutes to 1 hour
  • A veterinary check-up is required first
  • A personal quote, no commitment
